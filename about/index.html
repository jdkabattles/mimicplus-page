About mimicplus.

mimicplus is a delivery. You record an API you are allowed to call. The recording becomes a versioned spec, a typed Python client, OpenAPI, tests, and a check that fails when a later recording removes or renames a field. Secrets stay out of the spec. The price is $199 once.

Jonathan Battles builds it. This page is not a biography. The product is the subject. Mail goes to jdkabattles@gmail.com. The site is forgingstack.com.
