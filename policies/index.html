Privacy Policy — ForgingStack / Gold Is The Receipt

What we collect: When you buy a sample pack or join a waitlist, we collect your email address and the specs you provide at checkout (for example: Overture = Niche + Metro; Jobs = Category; SEC = Sector). If you email us, we receive what you send. For harwatch, if you submit a HAR file for a check, we receive that file for that check.

What we do NOT collect: We do not collect payment card details. Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store card numbers. We do not sell your personal data.

Tracking: We use Google and Microsoft tags on marketing pages only, to understand visits. We use zero tracking on tool pages — including Mimicplus and any page where you paste or upload files. We do not add tracking to tool pages, ever.

How we use what you send: To deliver what you bought, to reply to your email, to run a check you asked for, and to improve delivery. We do not use your HAR file or your specs for any other purpose.

Retention: Delivery emails and receipts are kept for order records. HAR files submitted for harwatch checks are deleted after the check and receipt are delivered, unless you ask us to keep them for a follow-up.

Sharing: We share only what is needed to operate: Stripe for payment, and hosting (GitHub Pages). We do not share your email or specs with advertisers or data brokers.

Contact: Questions: jdkabattles@gmail.com. Support is email-only, best-effort. No 24/7, no SLA.

Refund Policy

We refund when: Delivery was not made, or the delivery is broken due to our fault (for example: wrong product, unreadable file, missing rows that were promised, or a check that was paid for but never run).

We do not refund for: Change of mind after a correct delivery, data becoming outdated after the stated as-of date, a job closing after delivery, a market move after delivery, or a GOLD fail that was honestly earned.

harwatch: Cancel stops future charges. We refund a month if we fail to run a check you submitted in that month. We do not refund months with no submissions.
