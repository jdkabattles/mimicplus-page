Buy flow

  1. Choose a product on Products.
  2. Check out via Stripe. Your specs are collected at checkout (Overture: Niche, Metro; Jobs: Category; SEC: Sector).
  3. We run the delivery generator for your specs.
  4. We email you the CSV and the delivery receipt.
  5. The receipt records the delivery: hash, counts, filters. If the receipt does not match the CSV, the delivery is not proven.

How delivery works

Each delivery is a CSV plus a delivery receipt. Delivery is best-effort — no fixed target, no SLA promise.

Refund

If we do not deliver, or the delivery is broken because of our fault, we refund you.
